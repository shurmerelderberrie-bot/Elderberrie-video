Every December there's a moment when you realise the cards are still in the drawer and the post office queue is out of the door. If that's you, don't panic. Here are this year's dates, and what to do if you've already missed them.

## Royal Mail's last posting dates for Christmas 2026

For cards and letters sent within the UK, Royal Mail's recommended last posting dates for delivery before Christmas Day are:

- **2nd Class:** Friday 18 December
- **1st Class:** Monday 21 December
- **Special Delivery Guaranteed:** Wednesday 23 December

Cards to other countries need to go much earlier, from early December depending on where they're going. Check [Royal Mail's last posting dates page](https://www.royalmail.com/christmas/last-posting-dates) for the latest dates before you post, as they can change.

## How much does it cost to post a Christmas card?

Since 7 April 2026, a standard 1st Class stamp costs £1.80 and a 2nd Class stamp costs 91p. If you're sending twenty cards, that's £18.20 in 2nd Class stamps or £36 in 1st Class, before you've bought a single card.

## Missed the post? Four ways to still get a card there

1. **Hand deliver it.** For neighbours, colleagues, teachers and family nearby, a card through the door on Christmas Eve is lovely.
2. **Take it with you.** If you're visiting on Christmas Day, bring the card and the present together.
3. **Send a photo of the card.** For someone far away, write the card, take a photo and send it by message, then post the real one for them to keep.
4. **Print one tonight.** A printable card is ready the moment you buy it, so there's no post to wait for.

Many local libraries offer printing for a small charge per page, and so do print and copy shops. Bring the PDF on your phone or email it to yourself, and ask for it to be printed on card at actual size.

## Questions people ask

### What is the last day to post a Christmas card in 2026?

For delivery in the UK before Christmas, Royal Mail recommends Friday 18 December for 2nd Class and Monday 21 December for 1st Class. Special Delivery Guaranteed runs until Wednesday 23 December, but it costs more.

### Is it rude to give a Christmas card after Christmas?

Not at all. Plenty of people send cards between Christmas and New Year. A card that says "Merry Christmas and a happy new year" covers you either way.

### Do printable cards look homemade?

Printed on good A4 card at your printer's best quality setting, they look like a shop-bought card. Choose matte card for the richest colours.

### Can I print the same card more than once?

Yes. With our cards you can print as many as you need for your own family, friends and colleagues. Please don't resell or share the files.
