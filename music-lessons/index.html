        Good to know
        
The small print

        
Plain and simple, and always open to a conversation.

      

      

        

          Booking and payment
          

            
Once we've agreed a time, I'll email you a booking confirmation with the date, place, price and payment details.

            
Lessons and tasters are paid by bank transfer before the lesson. Regular students can pay monthly in advance if that's easier.

            
Worship team sessions are invoiced after the session and payable within 14 days.

          

        

        

          Cancellations and changes
          

            
Need to cancel or move a lesson? Let me know at least 24 hours before and we'll rearrange it, or I'll refund you. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are usually charged, though I'll always try to be fair.

            
If I have to cancel, you'll get a new time or a full refund.

            
When you book, you also have 14 days to change your mind and get a refund. If you ask to start lessons within those 14 days and then cancel, you pay only for the lessons you've already had.

          

        

        

          Lessons for under-18s
          

            
Lessons for under-18s are booked by a parent or guardian, and I keep in touch with you rather than messaging your child directly.

            
For lessons at home, a parent or guardian should be in the house, and you're always welcome to sit in. For online lessons, please be nearby.

          

        

        

          Your privacy
          

            
This page doesn't store anything you type. Your enquiry only reaches me when you send it from your own email.

            
I use your details only to reply to you and to arrange and run your lessons or sessions, and I keep the payment records the law requires. I don't share your details or add you to a mailing list.

            
You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete your details. If you're unhappy with how I've handled them, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
