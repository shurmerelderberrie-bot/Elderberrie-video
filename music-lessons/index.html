About me

Hi, I'm Joseph

I started leading worship when I was 12, and I've been playing, singing and leading worship teams ever since. That's nearly 20 years of music, most of it spent helping other people play and sing their best.

I taught myself guitar, so I know exactly which parts feel hard at the start and how to get past them. I'm vocally trained and I put a lot of weight on proper breathing, because it's what keeps a voice strong and healthy. I've also been teaching students for a good few years.
